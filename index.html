About

Poet, Translator and Transmedia Artist based in Helsinki. His work spans poetry, essay, sculptural installation, graphic design, event production, mixology, and culinary research.

Recurring interests: language as material, semantic archeology, durational systems, the politics of hospitality, ecological displacement, and speculative ecologies.

Currently developing Larga Esquina de Verano, a research and production grant for artistic development by the Kone Foundation. This artistic research is grounded in themes of migrating ecologies, diaspora, and ecological displacement.
